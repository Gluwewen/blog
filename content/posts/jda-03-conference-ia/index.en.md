It was with a mix of pride—and, I’ll admit, a touch of nervousness—that I accepted the invitation from [Madame Tseng](https://www.linkedin.com/in/catherine-tseng-79b393161/), head of school at [lycée Jeanne d’Arc in Cessy](https://www.linkedin.com/company/institut-jeanne-d-arc-gex/), to deliver the **opening keynote for the pedagogical day** on April 18, 2025, focused on **Artificial Intelligence**.

After a friendly breakfast, I spoke to an audience of around **70 high school teachers**—a meaningful moment to kick off the day’s reflections on a topic as vast as it is vital.

During the talk, we explored:
- What artificial intelligence (really) is
- A brief overview of its history and underlying technologies
- The promises and benefits of its applications
- **Its environmental impact**, along with **ethical and geopolitical challenges**

The audience's attention was tangible. **Questions came quickly**, 33discussions were rich**, and the **collective energy** was truly inspiring. One thing was clear: there is a strong need for understanding, perspective, and tools to help address this topic with students.

In a world increasingly shaped by AI, I believe it’s essential for **the education system to fully embrace the topic**—so young people can grow into informed, critical citizens.

Many thanks to **Madame Tseng** for her trust, and to the entire team at Lycée Jeanne d’Arc for the warm welcome.

And of course, heartfelt gratitude to [SoKube](https://www.sokube.io), for supporting and encouraging these meaningful spaces of dialogue—where they matter most: in learning environments.
