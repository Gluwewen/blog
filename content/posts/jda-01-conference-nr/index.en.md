A big thank you to [Mr. Duvivier](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sebastien-duvivier-161595266/), principal of [Jeanne d’Arc Middle School in Gex](https://www.linkedin.com/company/institut-jeanne-d-arc-gex/), and his deputy Ms. Sintès, for their trust and warm welcome regarding my proposal to raise awareness about responsible digital practices.

We decided to start at the very beginning: **by raising awareness among the teachers**. Why? Because they are the ones who, every day, shape the minds and imaginations of our youth. And their involvement is key to fostering a culture of digital responsibility within the school.

**On February 10, 2025**, around twenty teachers gathered for a two-hour participatory conference. On the agenda:
- An introduction to the environmental and social impacts of digital technologies
- Debunking common “false good ideas”
- Concrete actions that can be taken at both individual and educational levels
- And finally, a dive into future challenges:
  - A decarbonized yet highly mineral-dependent world
  - The depletion of non-renewable resources
  - Geopolitical tensions surrounding #Data
  - #AI: a powerful yet energy-hungry tool that must be better understood and managed

The discussions were rich, the questions plentiful, and the curiosity palpable. Most importantly: the desire to take action was real. A few days later, I learned that several teachers had been deeply impacted by the session. To me, that’s the best reward there is.

Next step: co-designing a **90-minute course on responsible digital use** for the school’s **eight 8th-grade classes**, in connection with their Technology curriculum. But more on that in the next article!

Once again, a huge thank you to the Jeanne d’Arc Middle School and its teaching staff for the warm welcome and genuine engagement. I can’t wait to be back for the next phase!

And of course, heartfelt thanks to [SoKube](https://www.sokube.io), my company, for encouraging and allowing me to dedicate time to such meaningful and impactful initiatives.
